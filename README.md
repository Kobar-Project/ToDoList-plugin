KoBar is a versatile platform designed to host a rich ecosystem of plugins, seamlessly integrating powerful tools directly into your daily workflow. As a core extension for this ecosystem, the **To-Do List** plugin transforms KoBar into an ultra-accessible task management center.

## What is To-Do List?

The To-Do List plugin provides a minimal, distraction-free widget right on your screen's edge. Designed for quick task dumping, it ensures that your most critical daily objectives are always visible. No heavy interfaces, no complex project boards—just a fast, sequential list of things to get done today.

## Key Features

- **Draggable UI:** Detach the list from the sidebar and place it anywhere on your desktop for continuous visibility.
- **Sequential Priority:** Easily drag and drop individual tasks to reorder them based on current priorities.
- **Progress Tracking:** A sleek progress bar visualizes your completion rate as you check off items throughout the day.
- **Local Persistence:** Tasks are securely saved to your local disk, ensuring your list survives system reboots or KoBar restarts.
- **KoCalendar Integration:** Syncs seamlessly with the KoCalendar plugin; any task given a deadline automatically appears in the calendar's Agenda view.
- **Smart Cleanup:** One-click functionality to instantly clear out all completed tasks.

## Configuration & Usage

1. **Open the List:** Click the To-Do icon in the KoBar sidebar to open the task view.
2. **Add Tasks:** Type into the input field at the bottom and press Enter to instantly add a new item.
3. **Reorder:** Click and drag the handle next to any task to move it up or down the priority queue.
4. **Complete:** Check the box next to an item to strike it out and update your daily progress bar.
5. **Float Mode:** Use the widget drag handle to pull the list out of the sidebar bounds to turn it into a floating sticky note.
